## Overview

Obsidian is a Markdown-based note-taking app with a strong plugin ecosystem. These are the plugins I actually use daily.

## 1. [Calendar](https://github.com/liamcain/obsidian-calendar-plugin)

Calendar shows your daily notes on a visual calendar. Click a date to open or create that day's note. Handy for journaling, habit tracking, or just knowing what you wrote when.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/liamcain/obsidian-calendar-plugin/master/images/screenshot-full.png)

## 2. [Obsidian Excalidraw Plugin](https://github.com/zsviczian/obsidian-excalidraw-plugin)

Excalidraw lets you draw diagrams and flowcharts right inside Obsidian. The hand-drawn style makes them feel less formal, which is good for brainstorming and quick visual explanations.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/xol3teriwc3iskcc7q5p.png)

## 3. [Obsidian Git](https://github.com/denolehov/obsidian-git)

Obsidian Git auto-commits your vault on a schedule and can push to a remote. If you version-control your notes, this saves you from remembering to commit manually.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/denolehov/obsidian-git/master/images/source-view.png)

## 4. [Obsidian Mind Map](https://github.com/james-tindal/obsidian-mindmap-nextgen)

Mind Map turns your Markdown headings into a visual mind map. Good for seeing the structure of a long note at a glance, or for studying where connections between topics matter.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/james-tindal/obsidian-mindmap-nextgen/main/images/mind-map-checkboxes.png)

## 5. [Commander](https://github.com/phibr0/obsidian-commander)

Commander adds custom buttons to your Obsidian UI for any command. One click or hotkey to run a command without opening the command palette.

![](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/46250921/180301683-080256c4-84f9-4a2f-9b1c-f97af694683e.gif)

## 6. [Share Note](https://github.com/alangrainger/share-note)

Share Note generates a public URL for any note. The receiver sees a rendered version in their browser without needing Obsidian.

![](https://share.note.sx/file/notesx/files/0r9ijore47sn9hg2a28k.png)

## 7. [Obsidian Plugin Update Tracker](https://obsidian.md/plugins?id=obsidian-plugin-update-tracker)

Plugin Update Tracker shows which of your plugins have updates available. Update them all from one screen instead of checking each one individually.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/gykpuxqfm58c9acjhj06.png)

## 8. [Better Word Count](https://github.com/lukeleppan/better-word-count)

Better Word Count shows word counts per note, per heading, and per section. Useful when you're writing something with a target length.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/h8oy5k25ye6s3s30q8mb.png)

## 9. [Recent Files Obsidian](https://github.com/tgrosinger/recent-files-obsidian)

Recent Files shows your recently opened notes in the sidebar. Quick access without digging through folders, especially useful in large vaults.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/4zrbq7y7ui8qrumj8al5.png)

## 10. [Iconize](https://obsidian.md/plugins?id=obsidian-icon-folder)

Iconize lets you assign custom icons to folders. A small visual change that makes navigation faster when your vault has many folders.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/gzt9jflc9ta7mpa7043c.png)

## 11. [Obsidian Tasks Plugin](https://github.com/obsidian-tasks-group/obsidian-tasks)

Obsidian Tasks adds due dates, priorities, and recurrence to task checkboxes in your notes. Query tasks across your vault with a simple syntax.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/obsidian-tasks-group/obsidian-tasks/main/docs/images/acme.png)

## Conclusion

These plugins cover the gaps Obsidian leaves out of the box: calendars, diagrams, Git, mind maps, task management, and more. Install the ones that match your workflow, and ignore the rest.
